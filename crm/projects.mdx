Projects is your jobs pipeline. Pipeline value on **Overview** uses project data (open and won value).

Open **Projects** under Manage in the sidebar at `app.omnilocal.com/{org-slug}/...`.

## Board and table

Switch between **Board** and **Table** views to work the same jobs in a kanban layout or a list.

## Pipeline stages

Jobs move through these stages:

1. **New**
2. **Contacted**
3. **Qualified**
4. **Proposal Sent**
5. **Closed Won** / **Closed Lost**

Stage definitions for Contacts vs Companies are managed in [Types and stages](/crm/types-and-stages).

## Add a job

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Projects">
    In the sidebar under Manage, go to **Projects**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add job">
    Select **Add job** and enter the job details.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Move through stages">
    Drag or update the job across **New → Contacted → Qualified → Proposal Sent → Closed Won / Closed Lost**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Pipeline value

Projects contribute **open** and **won** value. Overview's pipeline value KPI updates from this data.

Keep job progress on Projects, and people records on [Contacts](/crm/contacts).
