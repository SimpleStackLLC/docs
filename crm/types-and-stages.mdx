title: "Types and stages"
description: "Configure contact types and pipeline stages for Contacts and Companies."
---

Settings lets you configure **contact types** and **stages** so Contacts and Companies use the labels your team needs.

Open these controls from [Settings](/account/settings) at `app.omnilocal.com/{org-slug}/...`.

## Contact types

Under Settings, manage **Contact types**. Available types include:

| Type | Typical use |
| --- | --- |
| **Lead** | New prospect |
| **Buyer** | Buyer relationship |
| **Seller** | Seller relationship |
| **Past client** | Completed client |
| **Sphere** | Sphere of influence |
| **Referral partner** | Partner who sends referrals |
| **Vendor** | Vendor relationship |
| **Other** | Catch-all label |

These types appear when you [Add contact](/crm/contacts) and filter the contacts table.

## Stages: Contacts vs Companies

Configure **Stages** separately for:

- **Contacts** — people pipeline stages
- **Companies** — company pipeline stages

[Projects](/crm/projects) uses the jobs board stages **New → Contacted → Qualified → Proposal Sent → Closed Won / Closed Lost**. Keep org stage definitions consistent so the team uses the same language across CRM and projects.
