Contacts is your CRM for people tied to the organization and its locations.

Open **Contacts** under Manage in the sidebar, or use the **Contacts** tab on a [location detail](/locations/overview) page.

  <img src="/images/product/contacts.png" alt="Contacts CRM table with search, filters, and Add contact for organization people" />
</Frame>

## Add and find contacts

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Contacts">
    In the sidebar under Manage, go to **Contacts**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a contact">
    Select **Add contact** and enter the person's details.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Search, filter, and sort">
    Use search, filters, and column sorting to find people quickly in the contacts table.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Contact types

Contact types are configured in Settings. Default types include:

- Lead
- Buyer
- Seller
- Past client
- Sphere
- Referral partner
- Vendor
- Other

Manage labels under [Types and stages](/crm/types-and-stages).

## Pair with Inbox and Projects

- Capture people from [Inbox](/crm/inbox) activity (reviews, leads, email)
- Move work forward on the [Projects](/crm/projects) pipeline board
