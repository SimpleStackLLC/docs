Inbox collects **reviews**, **leads**, and **email** across your locations so your team can respond in one place.

Open **Inbox** under Manage in the sidebar at `app.omnilocal.com/{org-slug}/...`. You can also open the **Inbox** tab on a [location detail](/locations/overview) page for location-scoped activity.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/product/inbox.png" alt="Inbox showing reviews, leads, and email activity across locations in one stream" />
</Frame>

## What appears in Inbox

| Source | What you get | Needs |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Reviews** | Google review activity | GBP connected |
| **Leads** | Local Services Ads leads | GLSA connected |
| **Email** | Email messages | Email connected |

## Empty until connected

Inbox stays empty until the relevant sources are connected:

- Connect [Google Business Profile](/getting-started/connect-google-business-profile) for review activity
- Connect [Google Local Services Ads](/getting-started/connect-google-local-services-ads) for leads
- Connect email for email messages

<Note>
  Overview KPIs follow the same connections: average rating needs GBP, and new leads need GLSA.
</Note>

## Work the stream

Use Inbox to keep reputation and lead responses in one stream, then continue follow-up in [Contacts](/crm/contacts) and [Projects](/crm/projects).
