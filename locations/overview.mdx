Locations are the core unit in OmniLocal. Rankings, reviews, AI Visibility, and many Overview KPIs attach to each business location.

Signed-in URLs always include your org slug: `app.omnilocal.com/{org-slug}/...`.

## Locations list

Open **Locations** in the sidebar to see every location in the organization.

| Column | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Name** | Location display name |
| **Address** | Business address |
| **Industry** | Industry assigned to the location |
| **Status** | Current location status |
| **Connections** | Connected sources such as GBP and Local Service Ads |
| **Last Synced** | When data last synced for the location |

## Add a location

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Locations">
    In the sidebar, go to **Locations**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Add Location">
    Select **Add Location** and enter the location details.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm in the table">
    The new location appears with **Name**, **Address**, **Industry**, **Status**, **Connections**, and **Last Synced**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

For a first-time walkthrough, see [Add your first location](/getting-started/add-your-first-location).

### Find your Business Profile

In **Add Location**, choose **Find my Business Profile** to look up your Google Business Profile by name.

1. Enter your business name.
2. Optionally, type a city in **Narrow by city**. You can pick a suggestion from the list, or just type the city the way you'd write it, such as `Farmville, VA` or `Farmville, Virginia`, and select **Search**.
3. When a city is applied, a **Near [city, state]** chip appears. Clear the chip to remove the city and search by name only.

If the city you typed matches more than one place (for example, a city name used in several states), OmniLocal uses the one in a state where your organization already has locations. If it still can't tell, it asks you to pick a city from the list instead of searching without it. If no city matches, check the spelling, or clear the box to search by name only.

<Tip>
  Adding a branch or second office? Searching by name alone favors a brand's best-known listing, so a newer location with few reviews may not show up. If you only see one result, add that location's city in **Narrow by city** and search again.
</Tip>

## Location detail

Open a location to manage its profile and connections:

- **Location Info** — core business details
- **GBP** — Google Business Profile connection status
- **Local Service Ads** — optional GLSA connection status when you use Local Service Ads
- **Geo Target** — geographic targeting for the location

Use the location **Rankings** tab for position data scoped to that location.

From location detail you can also **Edit**, **Archive**, or **Delete** the location.

<Note>
  **Local Service Ads** (GLSA) may appear on location detail when relevant. Connecting it can surface lead-related signals on Overview and enrich GLSA columns in [Markets](/rankings/markets). It is optional — not part of the Getting Started path.
</Note>

## Overview KPI

On **Overview**, the locations count updates as you add locations. Other Overview cards depend on connections and data:

| KPI | Needs |
| --- | --- |
| Average rating | GBP connected |
| New leads | Local Service Ads connected (when used) |

<Note>
  Connect Google Business Profile so ratings and reviews can flow into Overview and Reviews.
</Note>
