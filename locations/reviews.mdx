title: "Reviews"
description: "Track Google reviews, replies, competitors, and removed reviews."
---

Reviews shows your Google review timeline, velocity, missing or removed reviews, and competitor context. Syncing reviews requires a [Google Business Profile](/getting-started/connect-google-business-profile) connection.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/product/reviews.png" alt="Reviews page showing Google review timeline, velocity, and reply controls" />
</Frame>

## Sync and goals

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Reviews">
    In the sidebar, go to **Reviews**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sync reviews">
    Select **Sync reviews** to pull the latest Google reviews and replies. GBP must be connected.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Manage goals">
    Select **Manage goals** to set review targets. Goals also live under [Goals and notifications](/account/goals-and-notifications) in Settings.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Review tabs

| Tab | What you see |
| --- | --- |
| **Timeline** | Google reviews and replies over time |
| **Competitors** | Competitor review context |
| **Removed** | Reviews that were removed or are missing |

Use the timeline to monitor velocity and reply to Google reviews from the same view.

## Overview and Inbox

- Average rating on **Overview** needs GBP connected
- Review activity also appears in [Inbox](/crm/inbox) once GBP is connected

<Note>
  Notifications for new reviews are marked coming soon in Settings. Ranking report notifications are available today — see [Goals and notifications](/account/goals-and-notifications).
</Note>
