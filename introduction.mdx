OmniLocal is a multi-location local SEO and reputation platform for businesses and real estate agents. Sign in at [app.omnilocal.com](https://app.omnilocal.com), pick an organization, and work from `app.omnilocal.com/{org-slug}/...`.

Use one workspace to manage locations, rankings, reviews, markets, AI visibility, inbox, contacts, projects, citations, and billing.

  <img src="/images/product/overview.png" alt="OmniLocal Overview dashboard showing organization KPIs for locations, ratings, leads, and pipeline" />
</Frame>

## What you get

| Area | What it covers |
| --- | --- |
| **Overview** | Org at a glance — locations count, average rating (needs GBP), new leads (needs GLSA), pipeline value (needs projects) |
| **Locations** | Business locations, connections, and per-location detail |
| **Rankings** | Position tracking across locations |
| **Reviews** | Google reviews timeline, replies, competitors, removed reviews |
| **Markets** | Market Explorer for US cities and counties |
| **AI Visibility** | What ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Google AI Mode say about a location |
| **Inbox** | Reviews, leads, and email across locations |
| **Contacts** | CRM people |
| **Projects** | Jobs pipeline board |
| **Citations** | Directory listings and NAP consistency |
| **Billing & Settings** | Plans, members, goals, notifications, stages, phones |

<Note>
  **Agent** appears in the sidebar Tools section and currently shows "This section is coming soon."
</Note>

## Organizations

Use the org switcher in the app to move between organizations, or choose **Add organization**. Signed-in URLs always include your org slug: `app.omnilocal.com/{org-slug}/...`.

There is also a Feedback beta dialog in the product for sending product feedback.
