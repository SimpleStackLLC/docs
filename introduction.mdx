---
title: "What is OmniLocal"
description: "OmniLocal helps multi-location businesses manage locations, rankings, reviews, markets, and AI visibility from one workspace."
---

OmniLocal is a multi-location local SEO and reputation platform. Sign in at [app.omnilocal.com](https://app.omnilocal.com) and use one workspace to manage **Overview**, **Locations**, **Rankings**, **Reviews**, **Markets**, **AI Visibility**, plus **Billing** and **Settings** under Tools.

## What you get

| Area | What it covers |
| --- | --- |
| **Overview** | Org at a glance — locations count, average rating (needs GBP), and other live KPIs |
| **Locations** | Business locations, connections, and per-location detail |
| **Rankings** | Your tracked search positions across locations |
| **Reviews** | Google reviews timeline, replies, competitors, and removed reviews |
| **Markets** | Market Explorer research for US cities and counties |
| **AI Visibility** | What ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Google AI Mode say about a location |
| **Billing & Settings** | Plans, organization, goals, and ranking report notifications |

## Organizations

Use the org switcher in the app to move between organizations, or choose **Add organization**. Signed-in URLs always include your org slug: `app.omnilocal.com/{org-slug}/...`.

There is also a Feedback beta dialog in the product for sending product feedback.
