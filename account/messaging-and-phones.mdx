**Phone numbers** and **Messaging compliance** settings control how your organization handles messaging in OmniLocal.

Configure both under [Settings](/account/settings) for the current organization at `app.omnilocal.com/{org-slug}/...`.

## Phone numbers

Use the **Phone numbers** tab in Settings to manage organization phone numbers used across location and messaging workflows.

Keep NAP (name, address, phone) consistent with [Citations](/locations/citations) and each [location](/locations/overview) profile.

## Messaging compliance

Use the **Messaging compliance** tab to review and maintain compliance settings for messaging in your organization.

These settings apply per organization — switch orgs from the org switcher if you manage more than one.

## Pair with Inbox and Contacts

- [Inbox](/crm/inbox) consolidates reviews, leads, and email once those sources are connected
- [Contacts](/crm/contacts) store people your team messages or follows up with
