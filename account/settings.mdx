Settings covers organization setup and account configuration for OmniLocal.

Open **Settings** from the app, or use the org switcher to switch organizations / **Add organization**. Signed-in URLs use `app.omnilocal.com/{org-slug}/...`.

## Organization

Under **Organization** you can manage:

| Area | What it covers |
| --- | --- |
| **General** | Organization profile and general settings |
| **Members** | Who has access to the organization |

For sign-in and switching orgs, see [Sign in and organizations](/getting-started/sign-in-and-organizations).

## Configuration tabs

Settings also includes:

| Tab | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| **Goals** | Targets used across Reviews and Overview KPIs |
| **Notifications** | Ranking reports; new reviews / new leads coming soon |
| **Contact types** | Labels such as Lead, Buyer, Seller, and more |
| **Stages** | Stages for Contacts vs Companies |
| **Phone numbers** | Organization phone numbers |
| **Messaging compliance** | Messaging compliance controls |
| **Billing** | Plans and one-time services |

Deep dives:

- [Goals and notifications](/account/goals-and-notifications)
- [Messaging and phones](/account/messaging-and-phones)
