Settings covers organization setup and the account options available in the live OmniLocal customer product.

Open **Settings** from Tools in the sidebar, or use the org switcher to switch organizations / **Add organization**. Signed-in URLs use `app.omnilocal.com/{org-slug}/...`.

## Organization

Under **Organization** you can manage:

| Area | What it covers |
| --- | --- |
| **General** | Organization profile and general settings |
| **Members** | Who has access to the organization |

If an agency manages your organization, its people aren't listed under Members and don't use member seats. Admins see a note there with a **See agency access** link. See [Agency access](#agency-access) below.

For sign-in and switching orgs, see [Sign in and organizations](/getting-started/sign-in-and-organizations).

## Goals

Configure **Goals** in Settings to align targets with Overview and Reviews KPIs.

From [Reviews](/locations/reviews), you can also open **Manage goals** to set review targets without leaving the reviews workflow.

## Notifications

| Notification | Status |
| --- | --- |
| **Ranking reports** | Available — turn on in Settings → Notifications |

Use ranking report notifications to stay on top of [Rankings](/rankings/overview) changes across locations.

<Note>
  Review and lead alert notifications are not available yet. Monitor reviews in [Reviews](/locations/reviews).
</Note>

## Billing

Open the **Billing** tab in Settings, or go to **Billing** under Tools, to manage plans and one-time services. See [Billing](/account/billing) for plan details.

## Agency access

If you work with an agency, its staff can work in your organization without becoming members. Each person gets a role from the agency that limits what they can see and change. **Settings → Agency access** shows you who they are.

<Note>
  Only your organization's admins can see this page, pause an agency, or remove one. Agency staff never see it.
</Note>

For each agency, the page shows:

- Whether access is **Active** or **Paused**, and how long the agency has worked with you.
- **People with access**, with each person's name, title, and email. Their role is shown as a badge; hover it for what the role covers. You'll also see **All clients** if their role covers every client of that agency, **Until** a date if their access expires, and **Invite pending** if they haven't accepted yet.

If no agency has access, the page says so. An agency appears here once it starts managing your organization.

### Pause an agency

Turn on **Pause agency access** and confirm. Everyone from that agency loses access right away, including anyone working in your organization at that moment. Their roles are kept, so when you turn the switch off and choose **Resume access**, they get back the access they had before.

### Remove an agency

Choose **Remove agency** and confirm. Everyone from that agency loses access right away, and the roles they were given in your organization are deleted. To work with you again, the agency has to add your organization as a client again.
