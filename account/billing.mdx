Billing is priced per active location. You can also purchase one-time services such as review removal and GBP reinstatement.

<Note>
This guide is a stub. Full walkthrough coming next.
</Note>

## What you can do here

- Open **Billing** under Tools or from Settings → Billing
- Choose a plan: OmniCore Free, OmniUltra $300/mo, OmniLegend $600/mo
- Pick monthly or annual billing
- Purchase one-time services (review removal, GBP reinstatement)
- Understand pricing is per active location
