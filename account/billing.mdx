Billing is priced **per active location**. Open **Billing** under Tools in the sidebar, or from [Settings](/account/settings) → **Billing**.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/product/billing.png" alt="Billing page showing OmniCore, OmniUltra, and OmniLegend plans priced per active location" />
</Frame>

## Plans

| Plan | Price |
| --- | --- |
| **OmniCore** | Free |
| **OmniUltra** | \$300/mo |
| **OmniLegend** | \$600/mo |

Choose **monthly** or **annual** billing when you select a paid plan.

Pricing scales with each **active location** in the organization. Manage locations under [Locations](/locations/overview).

## One-time services

In addition to subscription plans, you can purchase one-time services:

- **Review removal**
- **GBP reinstatement**

These are separate from the monthly or annual plan charge.

## Where to manage billing

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Billing">
    Go to **Billing** under Tools, or open **Settings → Billing**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a plan">
    Select OmniCore, OmniUltra, or OmniLegend, then monthly or annual billing.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add one-time services if needed">
    Purchase review removal or GBP reinstatement when you need those services.
  </Step>
</Steps>
