**Goals** and **Notifications** live under [Settings](/account/settings) so you can track targets and choose which reports you receive.

## Goals

Configure **Goals** in Settings to align targets with Overview and Reviews KPIs.

From [Reviews](/locations/reviews), you can also open **Manage goals** to set review targets without leaving the reviews workflow.

## Notifications

| Notification | Status |
| --- | --- |
| **Ranking reports** | Available — turn on in Settings → Notifications |
| **New reviews** | Coming soon |
| **New leads** | Coming soon |

Use ranking report notifications to stay on top of [Rankings](/rankings/overview) changes across locations.

<Note>
  New reviews and new leads notifications are marked coming soon. Until then, monitor review and lead activity in [Reviews](/locations/reviews) and [Inbox](/crm/inbox).
</Note>
