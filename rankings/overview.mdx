**Rankings** tracks **your** locations' search positions so you can see what is **Improving**, **Stable**, or **Declining**. Use it for owned-location performance — not market research (see [Markets](/rankings/markets)) or AI assistant mentions (see [AI Visibility](/rankings/ai-visibility)).

Open **Rankings** in the sidebar at `app.omnilocal.com/{org-slug}/...`.

## Rankings at a glance

Use the date range control to focus on the period you care about. Rankings summarizes:

| Signal | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Tracked locations** | How many locations are being tracked |
| **Average position** | Overall position across tracked locations |
| **Best performer** | Strongest ranking location in the period |
| **Needs attention** | Locations that need follow-up |

Results segment into **Improving**, **Stable**, and **Declining** so you can prioritize work.

## Browse Markets

From Rankings, open **Browse Markets** to jump into [Market Explorer](/rankings/markets) for US cities and counties, including GLSA, Local Pack, and Maps research data.

## Work with locations

Rankings attach to business locations. Open a location's **Rankings** tab from [location detail](/locations/overview), or manage the org-wide Rankings view from the sidebar.
