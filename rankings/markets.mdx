**Market Explorer** is for **market research** — browse US cities and counties and compare reach, GLSA, Local Pack, and Maps signals. It is separate from tracking **your** locations in [Rankings](/rankings/overview) and from AI assistant mentions in [AI Visibility](/rankings/ai-visibility).

Open Markets from the sidebar, or jump here from Rankings with **Browse Markets**.

## Market Explorer table

Filter the list of US cities and counties, then review:

| Column | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **City** | City name |
| **County** | County name |
| **Reach** | Market reach signal |
| **Omni Rank** | Where your business ranks among all businesses ranked in that market, with your Omni score in points (for example, "#3 of 11 businesses") |
| **GLSA** | Your average Google Local Services Ads rank in that market |
| **Local Pack** | Your average Local Pack rank in that market |
| **Maps** | Your average Maps rank in that market |
| **Scanned** | Whether the market has been scanned |
| **Top 3** | Top 3 presence |

## Omni Rank

The **Omni Rank** column blends your GLSA, Local Pack, and Maps results into one score for each market, then shows where that score places you among every business ranked there.

- Hover the rank to see your Omni score, your rank out of the businesses in that market, and how much each search surface added.
- If the result comes from one of your listings based in another city, the tooltip says **via** that city.
- A dash means the market hasn't been scanned yet, or your business wasn't among the businesses ranked there. Hover it to see which.
- Sort by **Omni Rank** to put your best markets first. Equal ranks are ordered by the higher score.

If a location has both Local Service Ads and a Google Business Profile connected, OmniLocal counts them as one business in each market, so your LSA results add to that business's Omni score.

## Averaged ranks and the averaging window

The GLSA, Local Pack, Maps, and Omni Rank columns show the same numbers you see when you open that market. Ranks are averaged across every check in the chosen time window, in every scanned city, not only cities where you have a location.

Use the **averaging window** menu above the table to pick **Last 7 days**, **Last 30 days** (the default), or **Last 90 days**. The menu shows your choice, for example **30-day averages**, and the page link keeps it, so a shared or bookmarked link opens with the same window.

Hover a rank to see:

- How many checks the average covers (for example, "30d average over 12 checks")
- How often you appeared ("Seen in 75% of checks")
- Your rank in the latest scan
- "Not seen in any of 12 checks" if you never showed up in that window, or "No checks in the last 7 days" if the market wasn't checked in that window
- "Only 9 days of checks in this market so far" when the market has less history than the window you picked

A Local Services Ads listing that serves a wider area shows its average rank in every targeted city where it appears, labeled with its home city (for example, **via Denver**).

When you sort by a rank column, OmniLocal uses these averaged numbers for the scanned markets with the most reach first (up to 30). Markets past that are sorted by their list values.

## Market detail

Open a market to dig into detail tabs:

| Tab | Focus |
| --- | --- |
| **Overview** | High-level market summary |
| **GLSA** | Local Services Ads detail |
| **Local Pack** | Local Pack detail |
| **Maps** | Maps detail |

<Note>
  If a location has **Local Service Ads** connected, Overview can show lead-related signals and Markets can enrich GLSA-related columns. Connecting LSA is optional — see [Locations](/locations/overview).
</Note>
