**Market Explorer** is for **market research** — browse US cities and counties and compare reach, GLSA, Local Pack, and Maps signals. It is separate from tracking **your** locations in [Rankings](/rankings/overview) and from AI assistant mentions in [AI Visibility](/rankings/ai-visibility).

Open Markets from the sidebar, or jump here from Rankings with **Browse Markets**.

## Market Explorer table

Filter the list of US cities and counties, then review:

| Column | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **City** | City name |
| **County** | County name |
| **Targeting** | **Not targeted**, **Targeted**, or **Targeted** via the wider county or state target that covers it. Show or hide it under **Columns**. |
| **Reach** | Market reach signal |
| **GLSA** | Google Local Services Ads presence |
| **Local Pack** | Local Pack visibility |
| **Maps** | Maps visibility |
| **Scanned** | Whether the market has been scanned |
| **Top 3** | Top 3 presence |

## Target several markets at once

Organization admins can add many cities or counties to their target markets, or remove them, in one step:

1. Tick the checkbox at the start of each row you want. Hold **Shift** and click another row's checkbox to select every row between the two on that page.
2. To select the whole page, tick the checkbox in the table header. Once a full page is selected, click **Select all** to include every market in the current results, not just this page.
3. In the selection bar, click **Target** to add the selected markets to your target markets, or **Stop targeting** to remove them. When an action only applies to some of the selected rows, its button shows how many it will change. Click the **X** to clear the selection.

Good to know:

- Selected markets that are already targeted are skipped when you click **Target**.
- Rows covered by a wider county or state target are counted in the selection bar (for example, "1 via Jefferson County, CO") but aren't changed. To stop targeting them, remove the county or state target instead.
- Changing the state, county, search, or any filter clears your selection, so a bulk action only changes markets in the results you're looking at. Moving between pages keeps it.
- Each organization can have up to 500 targets. If adding your selection would go past that limit, none of it is added.

## Market detail

Open a market to dig into detail tabs:

| Tab | Focus |
| --- | --- |
| **Overview** | High-level market summary |
| **GLSA** | Local Services Ads detail |
| **Local Pack** | Local Pack detail |
| **Maps** | Maps detail |

<Note>
  If a location has **Local Service Ads** connected, Overview can show lead-related signals and Markets can enrich GLSA-related columns. Connecting LSA is optional — see [Locations](/locations/overview).
</Note>
