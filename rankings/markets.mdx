**Market Explorer** is for **market research** — browse US cities and counties and compare reach, GLSA, Local Pack, and Maps signals. It is separate from tracking **your** locations in [Rankings](/rankings/overview) and from AI assistant mentions in [AI Visibility](/rankings/ai-visibility).

Open Markets from the sidebar, or jump here from Rankings with **Browse Markets**.

## Market Explorer table

Filter the list of US cities and counties, then review:

| Column | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **City** | City name |
| **County** | County name |
| **Reach** | Market reach signal |
| **GLSA** | Google Local Services Ads presence |
| **Local Pack** | Local Pack visibility |
| **Maps** | Maps visibility |
| **Scanned** | Whether the market has been scanned |
| **Top 3** | Top 3 presence |

## Target markets

**Target markets** are the states, counties, and cities your organization wants to be visible in and serve, whether through ads or organically. Mark them in Market Explorer, then filter the list to see where you are targeting and where you are not.

### Filter by targeting

Use the targeting filter above the table:

| Option | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Any Targeting** | Every market (the default) |
| **Target Markets** | Only markets covered by one of your targets |
| **Not Targeted** | Only markets outside your targets |

The filter is saved in the page link, so you can bookmark or share a filtered view. If a filtered view comes up empty, Market Explorer lists the states where you do have targets so you can jump straight to them.

### Mark a target

- **A single city or county:** click the pin at the start of its row. A targeted row shows a green crosshair; click it again to remove that target.
- **A whole state or county:** pick a state (and optionally a county), then click **Target [state]** or **Target [county]** in the card header. Click **Targeting [name]** to remove it.

The card header also shows how many markets in the current view are targeted, and states that hold targets are marked in the state picker.

A state or county target covers every market inside it. Hover the crosshair on a covered row to see which target covers it, for example "via Virginia." A covered row's pin can't be clicked; to stop targeting it, remove the wider target instead.

<Note>
  Anyone in your organization can see target markets. Only organization admins can add or remove them. You can also manage the full list in [Settings → Target markets](/account/settings#target-markets).
</Note>

## Market detail

Open a market to dig into detail tabs:

| Tab | Focus |
| --- | --- |
| **Overview** | High-level market summary |
| **GLSA** | Local Services Ads detail |
| **Local Pack** | Local Pack detail |
| **Maps** | Maps detail |

<Note>
  If a location has **Local Service Ads** connected, Overview can show lead-related signals and Markets can enrich GLSA-related columns. Connecting LSA is optional — see [Locations](/locations/overview).
</Note>
