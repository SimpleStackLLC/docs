**Market Explorer** is for **market research** — browse US cities and counties and compare reach, GLSA, Local Pack, and Maps signals. It is separate from tracking **your** locations in [Rankings](/rankings/overview) and from AI assistant mentions in [AI Visibility](/rankings/ai-visibility).

Open Markets from the sidebar, or jump here from Rankings with **Browse Markets**.

## Market Explorer table

Filter the list of US cities and counties, then review:

| Column | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **City** | City name |
| **County** | County name |
| **Reach** | Market reach signal |
| **GLSA** | Google Local Services Ads presence |
| **Local Pack** | Local Pack visibility |
| **Maps** | Maps visibility |
| **Coverage** | A small dot grid showing where your business ranks across the market (see below) |
| **Scanned** | Whether the market has been scanned |
| **Top 3** | Top 3 presence |

### Coverage column

On markets where we check rankings across a grid of areas, **Coverage** shows one dot per area, colored by your business's typical (median) rank there across recent checks:

- **Green**: top 3
- **Amber**: 4 to 10
- **Red**: 11 or lower, or not found in that area

Hover the grid to see how many areas you're in the top 3, how many areas you were found in, and your city-search rank at the city center. If your business wasn't found in any of the areas checked, the dots are gray and the tooltip says so. The cell shows a dash when a market doesn't have grid data yet.

## Market detail

Open a market to dig into detail tabs:

| Tab | Focus |
| --- | --- |
| **Overview** | High-level market summary |
| **GLSA** | Local Services Ads detail |
| **Local Pack** | Local Pack detail |
| **Maps** | Maps detail |

### Maps column on grid-enabled markets

On markets with grid data, the **Maps** column in the business table shows each business's **city-search rank at the city center** (for example **#2**), with the number of areas it was found in underneath (for example **9 of 9 areas**). The badge is green for top 3, amber for 4 to 10, and red for 11 or lower. **20+** means the business isn't in the top 20 at the city center.

Hover the badge for more detail: top-3 areas, areas found, the "near me" 30-day average at the center, and when the grid was last checked. Sorting by Maps sorts by the city-center rank; businesses that aren't ranked at the center but appear in some areas come after, ordered by how many areas they're missing.

Markets without grid data keep the regular 30-day average rank in the Maps column.

<Note>
  If a location has **Local Service Ads** connected, Overview can show lead-related signals and Markets can enrich GLSA-related columns. Connecting LSA is optional — see [Locations](/locations/overview).
</Note>
