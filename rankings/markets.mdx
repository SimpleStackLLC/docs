**Market Explorer** lets you browse US cities and counties and compare reach, GLSA, Local Pack, and Maps signals.

Open Markets from the sidebar, or jump here from [Rankings](/rankings/overview) with **Browse Markets**.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/product/markets.png" alt="Market Explorer table listing US cities and counties with reach, GLSA, Local Pack, and Maps columns" />
</Frame>

## Market Explorer table

Filter the list of US cities and counties, then review:

| Column | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **City** | City name |
| **County** | County name |
| **Reach** | Market reach signal |
| **GLSA** | Google Local Services Ads presence |
| **Local Pack** | Local Pack visibility |
| **Maps** | Maps visibility |
| **Scanned** | Whether the market has been scanned |
| **Top 3** | Top 3 presence |

## Market detail

Open a market to dig into detail tabs:

| Tab | Focus |
| --- | --- |
| **Overview** | High-level market summary |
| **GLSA** | Local Services Ads detail |
| **Local Pack** | Local Pack detail |
| **Maps** | Maps detail |

<Note>
  Connecting [Google Local Services Ads](/getting-started/connect-google-local-services-ads) unlocks leads on Overview and Inbox, and enriches GLSA-related market data.
</Note>
