**Market Explorer** is for **market research** — browse US cities and counties and compare reach, GLSA, Local Pack, and Maps signals. It is separate from tracking **your** locations in [Rankings](/rankings/overview) and from AI assistant mentions in [AI Visibility](/rankings/ai-visibility).

Open Markets from the sidebar, or jump here from Rankings with **Browse Markets**.

## Market Explorer table

Filter the list of US cities and counties, then review:

| Column | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **City** | City name |
| **County** | County name |
| **Reach** | Market reach signal |
| **GLSA** | Google Local Services Ads presence |
| **Local Pack** | Local Pack visibility |
| **Maps** | Maps visibility |
| **Scanned** | Whether the market has been scanned |
| **Top 3** | Top 3 presence |

## Choose which states to browse

The state picker above the table lets you pick **any number of states**. Each state you pick shows as a short chip (for example **VA**). Clear every chip to browse **nationwide**.

Two shortcuts sit at the top of the state list:

- **All states** switches to a nationwide view.
- **Target states** picks every state that holds one of your organization's target markets in one click. It appears when your targets span more than one state.

States that hold target markets are also marked with a crosshair in the list.

### One state, several states, or nationwide

- **One state** works as before: you see every city or county in that state, can narrow it with the county picker, and can target the whole state or county from the card header.
- **Several states or nationwide** lists the **largest 1,000 markets** across them. The county picker and the target-a-whole-state button are hidden, and the County column includes the state, for example "Fairfax County, VA."

The card header names what you are looking at, such as "Largest 1,000 cities nationwide" or "12 cities in 2 states," and its scan-data and targeted counts only count the rows listed.

<Note>
  When you filter to **Target Markets** across several states or nationwide, Market Explorer lists every one of your targeted markets, even small towns outside the largest 1,000. Other filters, such as markets with visibility data, only look within the largest 1,000 markets in that view. Pick a single state to see all of its markets.
</Note>

### Which states open first

Market Explorer remembers the states you last picked for each organization. The first time you open it, it starts on the states that hold your target markets, then your organization's own state, and otherwise shows the whole country. Your state choice is saved in the page link, so you can bookmark or share a multi-state view.

## Market detail

Open a market to dig into detail tabs:

| Tab | Focus |
| --- | --- |
| **Overview** | High-level market summary |
| **GLSA** | Local Services Ads detail |
| **Local Pack** | Local Pack detail |
| **Maps** | Maps detail |

<Note>
  If a location has **Local Service Ads** connected, Overview can show lead-related signals and Markets can enrich GLSA-related columns. Connecting LSA is optional — see [Locations](/locations/overview).
</Note>
