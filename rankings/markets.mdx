**Market Explorer** is for **market research** — browse US cities and counties and compare reach, GLSA, Local Pack, and Maps signals. It is separate from tracking **your** locations in [Rankings](/rankings/overview) and from AI assistant mentions in [AI Visibility](/rankings/ai-visibility).

Open Markets from the sidebar, or jump here from Rankings with **Browse Markets**.

## Market Explorer table

Filter the list of US cities and counties, then review:

| Column | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **City** | City name |
| **County** | County name |
| **Reach** | Market reach signal |
| **GLSA** | Google Local Services Ads presence |
| **Local Pack** | Local Pack visibility |
| **Maps** | Maps visibility |
| **Scanned** | Whether the market has been scanned |
| **Top 3** | Top 3 presence |

## Sort the table

Click a column header to sort the whole list by that column, not just the page you're looking at. Click the same header again to flip the order. An arrow next to the header shows which column is sorted and in which direction.

Each column starts in the order you'd most likely want first:

| Column | First click shows |
| --- | --- |
| **City** and **County** | A to Z |
| **GLSA**, **Local Pack**, and **Maps** | Your best ranks first (#1 at the top) |
| **Reach**, **Coverage**, and **Targeting** | Highest first (targeted markets first for Targeting) |
| **Scanned** | Most recently scanned first |

The **Top 3** column can't be sorted.

A few things to know:

- **Empty cells stay at the bottom.** Markets that show a dash (no rank or no scan yet) stay below the rest whichever way you sort, so flipping a rank column shows your worst ranks first instead of a page of blanks.
- **Rank columns sort by the number you see.** That's your 30-day average rank when there is one, otherwise your rank from the latest scan.
- **Scanned sorts by how recently a market was scanned**, even though the column doesn't show the date.
- **Coverage sorts by the share of the map grid where you're in the top 3**, then by the share where you show up at all. The list may sit in its usual order for a moment while that data loads, then reorder. In a list with more than 30 scanned markets, only the 30 biggest-reach scanned markets get a Coverage value to sort by, and the rest sort as empty.
- **Ties** fall back to the biggest reach, then the name.
- Changing the sort takes you back to page 1. Any rows you've selected stay selected.
- The sort is saved in the page link, so it sticks when you reload and when you share the link. The default order is **Reach**, highest first.

## Market detail

Open a market to dig into detail tabs:

| Tab | Focus |
| --- | --- |
| **Overview** | High-level market summary |
| **GLSA** | Local Services Ads detail |
| **Local Pack** | Local Pack detail |
| **Maps** | Maps detail |

<Note>
  If a location has **Local Service Ads** connected, Overview can show lead-related signals and Markets can enrich GLSA-related columns. Connecting LSA is optional — see [Locations](/locations/overview).
</Note>
