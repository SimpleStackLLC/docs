**AI Visibility** shows how major AI assistants mention your brand and which sources they cite for a location. It is separate from traditional search tracking in [Rankings](/rankings/overview) (your positions) and from geographic research in [Markets](/rankings/markets).

## Assistants covered

OmniLocal checks visibility across:

- **ChatGPT**
- **Claude**
- **Gemini**
- **Google AI Mode**

## Run your first AI scan

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open AI Visibility">
    In the sidebar, go to **AI Visibility**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Run first AI scan">
    Select **Run first AI scan** for a location.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review results">
    Inspect **brand mentions** and **cited sources** returned by each assistant.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What to look for

| Signal | Why it matters |
| --- | --- |
| **Brand mentions** | Whether assistants name your business for relevant queries |
| **Cited sources** | Which pages or listings assistants point to |

AI Visibility attaches to locations, so keep [location profiles](/locations/overview) accurate to support stronger brand presence.
