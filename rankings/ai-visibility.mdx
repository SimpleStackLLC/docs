title: "AI Visibility"

---

AI Visibility shows how major AI assistants mention your brand and which sources they cite for a location. Use it alongside traditional [Rankings](/rankings/overview) to understand presence in AI answers.

## Assistants covered

OmniLocal checks visibility across:

- **ChatGPT**
- **Claude**
- **Gemini**
- **Google AI Mode**

## Run your first AI scan

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open AI Visibility">
    In the sidebar, go to **AI Visibility** (under Rankings & markets workflows).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Run first AI scan">
    Select **Run first AI scan** for a location.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review results">
    Inspect **brand mentions** and **cited sources** returned by each assistant.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What to look for

| Signal | Why it matters |
| --- | --- |
| **Brand mentions** | Whether assistants name your business for relevant queries |
| **Cited sources** | Which pages or listings assistants point to |

AI Visibility attaches to locations, so keep [location profiles](/locations/overview) and citations accurate to support stronger brand presence.

<Note>
  **Agent** in the sidebar Tools section is coming soon. AI Visibility scans are available today without Agent.
</Note>
