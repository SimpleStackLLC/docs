OmniLocal is organization-based. After you sign in, you work inside an org at `app.omnilocal.com/{org-slug}/...`.

## Sign in

Go to [app.omnilocal.com](https://app.omnilocal.com).

You can sign in with:

- **Email code** — enter your email and use the code sent to you
- **Google**
- **Apple**

## Organizations

Each organization has its own locations, rankings, reviews, CRM data, and billing.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the org switcher">
    Use the organization switcher in the app to see orgs you belong to.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Switch or add">
    Select an existing organization, or choose **Add organization** to create another.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the URL">
    After switching, the URL includes that org's slug: `app.omnilocal.com/{org-slug}/...`.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Organization settings

Open **Settings → Organization** to manage:

- **General** — org-level settings
- **Members** — who can access the organization

Other Settings tabs (Goals, Notifications, Contact types, Stages, Phone numbers, Messaging compliance, Billing) are covered under [Account & billing](/account/settings).

<Tip>
Need a second business or brand? Add another organization instead of mixing locations in one workspace.
</Tip>
