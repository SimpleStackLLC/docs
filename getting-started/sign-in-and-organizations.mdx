OmniLocal is organization-based. After you sign in, you work inside an org at `app.omnilocal.com/{org-slug}/...`.

## Sign in

Go to [app.omnilocal.com](https://app.omnilocal.com).

You can sign in with:

- **Email code** — enter your email and use the code sent to you
- **Google**
- **Apple**

## Accept an invitation

If someone added you to their organization, you get an invitation email.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the link">
    Open the link in the invitation email. You see an **Accept your invitation** page. Nothing happens until you click the button.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Accept invitation">
    OmniLocal signs you in, or creates your account if you don't have one yet.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start working">
    You land on the dashboard, inside the organization that invited you.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### If something goes wrong

- **Accepting fails**: the page shows what went wrong. Below it, under **or sign in another way**, you can continue with Google or Apple. Use the same email address that got the invitation.
- **Invitation link invalid**: the link is missing its invitation details. Open the link straight from the invitation email, or ask your admin to send the invitation again.
- **You're all set**: the invitation was already accepted. OmniLocal takes you to your dashboard.

## Organizations

Each organization has its own locations, rankings, reviews, markets, AI visibility, and billing.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the org switcher">
    Use the organization switcher in the app to see orgs you belong to.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Switch or add">
    Select an existing organization, or choose **Add organization** to create another.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the URL">
    After switching, the URL includes that org's slug: `app.omnilocal.com/{org-slug}/...`.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Organization settings

Open **Settings → Organization** to manage:

- **General** — org-level settings
- **Members** — who can access the organization

Other Settings areas (Goals, Notifications, Billing) are covered under [Settings](/account/settings).

<Tip>
Need a second business or brand? Add another organization instead of mixing locations in one workspace.
</Tip>
