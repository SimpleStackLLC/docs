Locations are the core unit in OmniLocal. Rankings, reviews, AI Visibility, and many Overview KPIs attach to locations.

## Add a location

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Locations">
    In the sidebar, go to **Locations**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Add Location">
    Select **Add Location** and enter the location details.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the Locations table">
    Your new location appears in the table with columns for **Name**, **Address**, **Industry**, **Status**, **Connections**, and **Last Synced**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Location detail

Open a location to see:

- **Location Info**
- **GBP** (Google Business Profile connection status)
- **Local Service Ads** connection status (optional)
- **Geo Target**
- **Rankings** tab for that location

## Overview KPI

On **Overview**, the locations count updates as you add locations. Other Overview cards depend on connections and data:

- Average rating needs **GBP**
- New leads need **Local Service Ads** when you use that connection

<Note>
After you add a location, connect Google Business Profile so ratings and reviews can flow into Overview and Reviews.
</Note>
