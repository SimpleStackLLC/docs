Connecting Google Local Services Ads (GLSA / Local Service Ads) unlocks new leads on Overview and lead activity in Inbox. Market Explorer also surfaces GLSA data for markets you browse.

## Why connect GLSA

| Area | What GLSA unlocks |
| --- | --- |
| **Overview** | New leads (needs GLSA) |
| **Inbox** | Lead activity across locations |
| **Location detail** | Local Service Ads linked status |
| **Markets** | GLSA column and market detail GLSA tab |

Without GLSA (and other sources), Inbox can remain empty for leads.

## Where connection status appears

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check Overview">
    Overview shows new leads when GLSA is connected and producing lead data.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open a location">
    Go to **Locations**, open a location, and check **Local Service Ads** linked status on the location detail page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Browse Markets">
    Open **Markets** (Market Explorer) to see the **GLSA** column for cities and counties, and open a market's **GLSA** tab for detail.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
This guide describes where OmniLocal shows Local Service Ads connection status and related data. Follow the in-app connect flow from the location's Local Service Ads section — exact connection screens may change.
</Warning>

## After you connect

- Watch **new leads** on Overview
- Work leads from **Inbox** alongside reviews and email
- Use Market Explorer filters and the GLSA column when researching markets
