---
title: "Connect Google Business Profile"
description: "Connect GBP to unlock ratings and Google review sync."
---

Connecting Google Business Profile (GBP) unlocks average rating on Overview and review sync on Reviews.

## Why connect GBP

| Area | What GBP unlocks |
| --- | --- |
| **Overview** | Average rating (needs GBP) |
| **Reviews** | Sync Google reviews and replies |
| **Location detail** | GBP connection status on the location |

Without GBP, Overview may prompt you to **Connect GBP**, and Reviews cannot sync Google data until the connection is in place.

## Where connection status appears

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check Overview">
    If GBP is not connected, Overview prompts you to **Connect GBP** so rating KPIs can populate.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open a location">
    Go to **Locations**, open a location, and review the **GBP** status on the location detail page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sync reviews">
    After GBP is connected, use **Sync reviews** on the Reviews page to pull Google reviews and replies.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
This guide describes where OmniLocal shows GBP connection status and prompts. Follow the in-app connect flow from Overview or the location's GBP section — exact OAuth screens may change.
</Warning>

## After you connect

- Use **Reviews** for timeline, velocity, missing/removed reviews, competitors, and Google replies
- Use **Manage goals** on Reviews when you are ready to set review goals
