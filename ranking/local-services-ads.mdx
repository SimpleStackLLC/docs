## Questions this page answers

- How do I rank higher on Local Services Ads?
- Why did my Local Services Ads position drop?

<Warning>
OmniLocal does not manage Local Services Ads. It cannot see your bid or your response time. Do not guess either number. If the app does not have a figure, say it is not in OmniLocal.
</Warning>

## An ad auction, not the free map

Local Services Ads are an ad auction. They are not the free local pack or the free Maps results. Google describes the auction in [About ad ranking for Local Services Ads](https://support.google.com/localservices/answer/7527305).

The highest-ranked ad is shown first. That placement is paid. It is separate from free map results. Free results are covered on [Local pack and Google Maps](/ranking/local-pack-and-maps).

## Bid

The bid is the most you will pay for a lead. You may pay less than that amount. Google recommends Maximize Leads when quality and budget are similar.

OmniLocal cannot see the bid. You set it in Google.

## Chance of a lead

Google estimates how likely the ad is to become a lead. Missed calls can hurt that estimate.

OmniLocal cannot see missed calls or that estimate. CallRail is not a customer feature. Do not invent a call count.

## The search

The auction looks at the search: the job, the time, and the place. It also looks at whether your services and description fit.

[Markets](/rankings/markets) only shows areas you track in OmniLocal. It does not show the searcher's job, the time, or the ad's service list.

## More ways to be contacted

More contact paths, such as messages and booking, can mean more leads when you cannot pick up. That matters on nights and weekends.

OmniLocal does not manage those paths. Inbox is not a live customer feature for this.

## Profile quality

Google points to profile quality: rating, review count, average response time, strong photos, and completed checks. A stronger profile may rank higher and cost less per lead.

OmniLocal can see review rating and review count in [Reviews](/locations/reviews). It cannot see response time, photos, or completed checks.

## Reviews on the Business Profile

Star rating and review count affect Local Services Ads order. Google explains that in [How reviews work with Local Services Ads](https://support.google.com/localservices/answer/7496631). You manage those reviews on the Business Profile. OmniLocal is not an ads manager.

## Answer quickly

Google says to answer quickly. See [Respond to customers in Local Services Ads](https://support.google.com/localservices/answer/6224859). OmniLocal cannot see response time. Do not guess it.

## Feedback from people who hired you

Serious or repeated negative feedback from people who hired you can lower your ranking or remove you. Google describes that in [About customer feedback in Local Services Ads](https://support.google.com/localservices/answer/6230381). OmniLocal has no complaint feed. Do not invent one.

## Two locations in one area

If you have two locations in one area, Google shows only the higher-ranked ad. Source: [About ad ranking for Local Services Ads](https://support.google.com/localservices/answer/7527305).

[Locations](/locations/overview) can list your places. It does not show which ad Google chose.

## Experiments

Google also experiments. A position change is not always something the business changed. Do not read every drop as a bid change or a slower response.

## What this page does not treat as auction factors

This page does not list review recency, how often you accept a job, call-script quality, or service-area size as auction factors. The official pages cited above do not name them that way.

## What people also ask about

People also ask about primary category, extra categories, business name keywords, an address in the city, pin placement, review recency, citation consistency, and website pages for a service and a city.

Google has not confirmed those as ranking factors on the official pages this guide cites. This page does not treat them as steps to take.
