## Questions this page answers

- What is the difference between the local pack and Google Maps?
- Why can my rank on Maps look different from my rank on Search?

Google does not publish two formulas for those two places. The factors Google does name are on [Rank higher on Google Maps](/ranking/google-maps).

## Two places, one profile

A local search can show a short list of nearby businesses on Google Search. It can also show a fuller map in Google Maps. The local pack is the small set of businesses and the map on a Search page. Maps is the map product.

Google does not publish a separate formula for the map block on Search versus the Maps app. Both use the Business Profile. Google describes local results in [Tips to improve your local ranking on Google](https://support.google.com/business/answer/7091).

One profile feeds both places. The name, address, hours, and other details on that profile are what Google can use on Search and in Maps. Google explains where those details come from in [How Google sources and uses information for local listings](https://support.google.com/business/answer/2721884).

Google also fills in facts the owner did not type. It can use a crawl of the business website, licensed data, and photos and reviews from users.

Hours can match an "open now" search. That is a match to the search. It is not a rule that a closed business always drops out.

You cannot buy a higher local spot. Google says there is no way to request or pay for a better local ranking. Ads are a different product. See [Rank higher on Local Services Ads](/ranking/local-services-ads).

## What OmniLocal shows

[Rankings](/rankings/overview) and [Markets](/rankings/markets) show a tracked position. They do not label that position as the local pack versus Maps. The rank is the outcome, not the cause.

[Locations](/locations/overview) shows whether a Google Business Profile is connected. A connection does not split your rank into a Search rank and a Maps rank.

<Note>
Screens that are not live for customers, including Inbox, Contacts, Projects, Citations, and Agent, cannot explain a rank. Do not invent a number the app does not have.
</Note>

## What people also ask about

People also ask about primary category, extra categories, business name keywords, an address in the city, pin placement, review recency, citation consistency, and website pages for a service and a city.

Google has not confirmed those as ranking factors on the official pages this guide cites. This page does not treat them as steps to take.
