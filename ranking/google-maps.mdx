## Questions this page answers

- How do I rank higher on Google or Maps?
- Does my review goal matter?
- Will answering reviews move my rank?
- Can I pay for a better map spot?

You cannot pay for a better free map spot. Google says there is no way to request or pay for a better local ranking. Ads are a different product.

Answering reviews is not a named ranking input on the pages this guide cites. Do not say that a reply will move the rank. Review replies are not a customer feature in OmniLocal.

Your review goal is your target in OmniLocal. Google talks about review count and positive ratings. It does not talk about that goal.

## What Google says

Google says local results are mainly based on relevance, distance, and popularity. It then explains that third factor as prominence. The details stay confidential. Source: [Tips to improve your local ranking on Google](https://support.google.com/business/answer/7091).

The local pack and the Maps app are two places those results can show. They are not two formulas. See [Local pack and Google Maps](/ranking/local-pack-and-maps).

## Relevance

Relevance is how well the profile matches the search. Complete, specific details help Google match the business to the query.

OmniLocal can show a rank for the searches you track in [Rankings](/rankings/overview). It cannot see categories, services, or attributes. Fix those in Google.

## Distance

Distance is how far the business is from the person searching.

[Locations](/locations/overview) has the business place. [Markets](/rankings/markets) has the areas you track. OmniLocal cannot see the searcher's distance. Do not move the pin or the address to look closer.

## Prominence

Prominence is how well known the place is. Google includes how many other sites link to the business.

Citations is not a customer feature. That work is on your website and on other sites. OmniLocal cannot see a citation count, so do not invent one.

## Reviews

Under prominence, Google points to review count and positive ratings, along with links from other sites.

[Reviews](/locations/reviews) can show counts and ratings. It cannot see the words inside reviews. [Settings](/account/settings) holds your goals. Ranking-report notifications can tell you when a tracked rank changes. The goal is your target. It is not itself a Google ranking input.

## A verified profile

Google says a verified profile is more likely to show in local results. Connecting a profile in [Locations](/locations/overview) is not the same as proving that Google has verified it. Check verification in Google.

## Incomplete details

An incomplete or inaccurate address, hours, category, or attributes can keep a profile out of searches it should match. Fix those in Google, not in OmniLocal. The app cannot see categories or attributes.

## Photos, videos, and products

Photos, videos, and retail products are official tips for the profile. Google does not name them as ranking factors on the page above. Treat them as profile quality. OmniLocal does not manage those files.

## Review replies

Google's [tips on reviews](https://support.google.com/business/answer/3474122) include replying as a trust tip. That is not a named rank input. Do not say that answering reviews will move the rank. Replies are not a customer feature in the app. OmniLocal can see review counts and ratings, not reply text.

## One next step

Use one next step from Rankings, Reviews, and Goals. Do not return a generic checklist.

Read the tracked position, the review count and rating, and the customer's goal. Name the single gap those numbers support. If the gap is a category, an attribute, a citation, a photo, or a reply, say the customer handles it outside OmniLocal. If the app does not have the number, say so.

## What people also ask about

People also ask about primary category, extra categories, business name keywords, an address in the city, pin placement, review recency, citation consistency, and website pages for a service and a city.

Google has not confirmed those as ranking factors on the official pages this guide cites. This page does not treat them as steps to take.
